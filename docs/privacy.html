Privacy Policy

BuildBuddy has no account service, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Sowens Studios does not receive your source, credentials, command output, or history through the app.

Local information

BuildBuddy stores preferences, project paths, action history, remote settings, and a random pairing token locally. It reads repositories and delivery files you select and can create commits, branches, logs, backups, archives, and results as directed. Credentials stay with Git, GitHub CLI, Keychain, or your configured helper.

Commands and providers

Commands such as Git, Xcode tools, package managers, and deployment CLIs may send data to GitHub, Apple, Cloudflare, registries, or hosts you configured. Their policies apply. Update checks contact GitHub.

Remote modes

Remote is off until enabled. LAN and Tailscale connect a paired device to your Mac; GitHub relay stores predefined jobs in your private repository; SMB reads a selected share; Public mode exposes a port and is higher risk. Anyone with the token and network access may trigger powerful actions. Prefer trusted networks and rotate exposed tokens.

Deletion and contact

Remove local preferences and Application Support files to clear BuildBuddy data. External repository information stays under each provider's controls. BuildBuddy has no central account. Contact support@sowensstudios.com or read the complete policy.
